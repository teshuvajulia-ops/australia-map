Latin Nights By The Bay

📅Fri 20 Nov 2026 – Fri 4 Dec 2026 · 18:00–22:00
📍Palm Grove (in front of IMAX), Darling Harbour, Sydney NSW

Every Friday 18:00-22:00 (until 4 Dec). Free, no booking needed. Open-air Latin dance night on the waterfront: a beginner salsa or bachata lesson, then DJs, live bands and social dancing. No partner or experience needed.
